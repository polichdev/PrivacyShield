# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in Privacy Shield development, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected Standards

- **Respect**: Treat contributors and community members with empathy, kindness, and professionalism.
- **Constructive Communication**: Focus on constructive feedback, technical merit, and shared goals.
- **Privacy & Security Focus**: Uphold the user privacy principles that define this project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, derogatory language, personal attacks, trolling, and publishing private information without explicit consent are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported directly to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially, leading to appropriate corrective actions up to and including removal from the project.
